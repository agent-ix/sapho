# US-006: Exact recording and offline replay

## Story

**As an** application author
**I want** to replay recorded judgments and inspect the steps that consume them
**So that** I can reproduce engine behavior without another inference call.

## Acceptance Examples (Illustrative)

- Given a declared input, when I compose the capability, then I receive inspectable results.
- Given invalid data or an unavailable required value, when I invoke it, then I can distinguish refusal from a completed result.

## Constraints (Contextual)

The first release is a Rust library; domain-specific rules remain downstream.

## Priority and Risk (Informative)

First-release priority. An ambiguous contract here can change a downstream interpretation silently.
