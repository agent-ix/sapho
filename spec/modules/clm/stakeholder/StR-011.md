# StR-011: Use provider-independent decisions securely

## Stakeholder Need

Sapho SHALL let a host choose a verified decision provider without putting credentials in decision documents or requiring live services for replay.

## Rationale

Operators need alternative providers while retaining inspectable probability evidence and reproducible offline evaluation.

## Validation Criteria

| ID | Criteria | Validation |
|----|----------|--------------|
| StR-011-VC-1 | Operators can evaluate through CLM and replay captured exchanges without credentials. | Demonstration |
